# Should the category team repeat the 35% spring promotion?

**Decision:** Pilot the promotion again in a subset of stores. The March 2024 comparison clears a commercial hurdle on total estimated contribution, but a full rollout requires a credible estimate of what the same products would have sold without the discount.

| Eligible US products, sales only | March 1–14 | March 15–28 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Units sold | 5,694 | 23,796 |
| Sales revenue | $259,445 | $724,463 |
| Estimated contribution after production cost | $179,162 | $375,461 |
| Estimated contribution per unit | $31.47 | $15.78 |
| Units sold at 35% off | 0% | 100% |

The promotion window sold about 4.18 times as many units. At the observed promotional contribution per unit, around twice the earlier volume would have been needed to match the earlier contribution. The observed volume cleared that threshold. Seasonality and changes in the mix of products sold could explain part of the movement. These figures describe the simulation.

**Next test.** Select comparable stores, randomize which receive the promotion, and compare contribution per store-day. Record returns, campaign spend, stock availability and the following two weeks of sales to check whether the discount pulled purchases forward.

**Where deeper discounts deserve scrutiny.** In Dresses and Jumpsuits, estimated contribution per unit was $39.92 at full price, $21.89 at 35% off and $11.98 at 50% off. Compare the same styles and weeks before approving a deeper markdown. The available groups have different product mixes.

**Inventory planning.** The previous week’s sales beat or tied the four-week mean on validation for all three categories. Across the eight test weeks its WAPE was 9.0% for Feminine, 9.6% for Masculine and 20.2% for Children. Use the baseline for a first planning estimate. Review the children’s category manually before issuing purchase orders, especially around promotions. The forecast for the week ending March 23, 2025, based on complete weeks through March 16, was 8,354, 8,445 and 2,926 units respectively.
